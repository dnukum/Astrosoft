# Muhurta Selection: 9-Step Auspicious Timing Workflow

This document details a  **9-step workflow** to identify, filter, evaluate, and lock in the most auspicious timing (*Muhurta*) for significant life events such as **Marriage (Vivaha)**, **House Warming (Grihapravesha)**, **Business Commencement**, **Travel**, and **Asset Acquisition**.

## 1. Overview & Screen Layout

Open the Muhurta module in Astrosoft by choosing **View &rarr; My Muhurta**. 

The interface is structured into three synchronized visual areas:
1. **Top Section - Main Muhurta Charts & Panchanga Table**:
   * **Panchanga & Muhurta Table (Left Column)**: Displays Date, Time, Location, Ayanamsa, Thithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana, Solar Vara, Auspicious Timings, and personal Balams. Below the table, **Edit Date/Time** adjusts the event instant/location, and **Summarize** opens a complete exportable text summary.
   * **Personal Horoscopes Toolbar (Right Column Top)**: Contains `[ Person 1: Select... ]` and `[ Person 2: Select... ]` buttons to load saved `.ash` horoscopes or use quick entry (Rasi & Nakshatra).
   * **Rasi Charts Strip (Right Column Center)**: Displays the **Muhurta Rasi Chart** computed for the exact event instant and location, placed side-by-side with natal **Person 1 Rasi** and **Person 2 Rasi** charts (when loaded).
   * **Calendar Toggle Button**: The **Show Balam Calendar** button directly below the Muhurta chart expands or collapses the Balam Calendar (enabled once Person 1 or Person 2 is selected).
2. **Middle Section - Monthly Balam Calendar Table**:
   * Displays transit Moon segments for the month with Tara and Chandra Balam scores for both partners.
   * Unlike a traditional Panchanga, this table is not organized on a daily (day-by-day) basis. Instead, it is organized by **transit segments of uniform Tara and Chandra Balam**. Within each segment, both the transit Moon's Nakshatra and Rasi remain unchanged, ensuring consistent Balam throughout. Consequently, you will see time segments that straddle across two civil calendar days, or multiple distinct segments occurring on the same calendar day.
   * **Navigation & Filter**: Month/Year selectors, Previous/Next Month arrows, and a sticky **Filter** button to eliminate inauspicious days.
3. **Bottom Section - 50% / 50% Two-Column Details View**:
   * **Left Column**: Start time, End time, Sunrise (if straddled), Transit Moon position, approved event activities for the active Nakshatra, and timed transitions for Thithi, Varam, Amirthathi Yoga, Nitya Yoga, and Karana.
   * **Right Column**: **Subha Hora** selection dropdown with **Update** button, full list of **Available** Subha Hora blocks, Abhijit Muhurta, and Brahma Muhurta intervals.

## 2. The 9-Step Muhurta Workflow

Follow these steps sequentially to evaluate and select an auspicious Muhurta:

```
Step 1: Tara Balam (Both partners; Wife highest priority)
   │
   ▼
Step 2: Chandra Balam (Both partners; No Ashtama Chandra)
   │
   ▼
Step 3: Nakshatram (1st Priority: Event List | 2nd Priority: Swathi, Chithirai, Magham)
   │
   ▼
Step 4: Thithi (Avoid Amavasya, Prathamai, Ashtami, Navami)
   │
   ▼
Step 5: Vara (Sunrise-to-sunrise; Avoid Tuesday & Saturday)
   │
   ▼
Step 6: Amirthathi Yoga (Amirtha/Siddha; Strictly avoid Marana)
   │
   ▼
Step 7: Yogam / Nitya Yoga (Auspicious combinations; Avoid Vishakambha, Vyatipata, Vaidhriti)
   │
   ▼
Step 8: Karnam / Karana (Avoid Vishti/Bhadra and inauspicious fixed karanas)
   │
   ▼
Step 9: Muhurtha Chart (Lagna strength, Ashtama Suddhi, Kendra benefics - **THE MOST CRUCIAL**)
```

---

### Step 1: Thara Balam (Tara Balam)

**Tara Balam** measures the compatibility between the transit Moon's Nakshatra and the individual's birth star (*Janma Nakshatra*).

#### The 9 Taras and Their Effects:
| Tara # | Sanskrit Name | Meaning / Classification | Auspiciousness |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | Janma | Birth star | Acceptable / Conditional (Paryaya 2 & 3 favorable) |
| **2** | Sampat | Wealth & Prosperity | **Best** |
| **3** | Vipat | Danger / Misfortune | Avoid (Acceptable only in 3rd Paryaya) |
| **4** | Kshema | Well-being & Protection | **Second Best** |
| **5** | Pratyak | Obstacles / Opposition | Avoid (Conditional for women in marriage) |
| **6** | Sadhana | Accomplishment of goals | **Best** |
| **7** | Naidhana | Destruction / Death-inflicting | **Strictly Rejected** |
| **8** | Mitra | Friendly / Helpful | **Second Best** |
| **9** | Parama Mitra | Supreme friend / Great fortune | **Best** |

#### Dual-Partner Evaluation & Priority Rules:
* **Marriage & House Warming (Grihapravesha)**: The **wife's / bride's Tara Balam carries the highest priority**. If the bride has an auspicious Tara (2, 4, 6, 8, 9), minor afflictions in the groom's Tara can be tolerated, but **never vice versa**.
* **Naidhana Tara (7)** is **strictly forbidden** for either partner.
* **Filter Feature**: Click the **Filter** button at the top right of the Balam Calendar. This automatically strips away all rows where either person has **Tara Balam = 7 (Naidhana) OR Chandra Balam = 8 (Ashtama)**.

---

### Step 2: Chandra Balam

**Chandra Balam** evaluates the transit Moon's strength relative to the individual's natal Moon sign (*Janma Rasi*).

#### House Positions from Natal Moon:
* **Best (Upachaya Houses)**: **3rd, 10th, 11th** houses from natal Moon (brings growth, victory, prosperity).
* **Acceptable**: **1st, 7th** houses.
* **Mixed / Contextual**: **2nd, 5th, 9th** houses.
* **Avoid**: 
  * **6th house**: Sickness/debt (some texts treat as Upachaya, but generally avoided for sacred ceremonies).
  * **12th house**: Expenditure, loss.
* **Strictly Rejected**: **8th house (Chandra Ashtama)**:
  * The transit Moon in the 8th house from one's natal Moon causes mental distress, obstacles, health vulnerability, and misfortune.
  * **Chandra Ashtama is strictly avoided** for the primary individual and partner.
* **Filter Feature**: The **Filter** button in the Balam Calendar enforces this rule alongside Step 1, simultaneously excluding any transit window where either person has **Chandra Balam = 8 (Ashtama) OR Tara Balam = 7 (Naidhana)**.

---

### Step 3: Nakshatram (Transit Moon's Star)

Once Tara and Chandra Balam are satisfied, evaluate the transit Nakshatra for your intended event.

Astrosoft does not require you to pre-select an event. Instead, when you click any window in the calendar, the details pane displays the active transit Nakshatra and automatically lists all classically approved activities for that star (e.g., *Marriage*, *House Warming*, *Vehicle Buying*, *Travel*, etc.). If a star is unsuitable for major milestones, it displays `<font color='red'>Not recommended</font>`. You evaluate whether the star supports the specific event you have in mind:

#### 1st Priority: Event-Specific Auspicious Nakshatras
Classical approved stars for major life events:
* **Marriage (Vivaha)**:
  * *Rohini, Mrigashirsha, Makha, Uttara Phalguni, Hasta, Swati, Anuradha, Moola, Uttara Ashadha, Uttara Bhadrapada, Revati*.
* **House Warming (Grihapravesha)**:
  * *Rohini, Mrigashirsha, Punarvasu, Pushya, Uttara Phalguni, Hasta, Chitra, Anuradha, Uttara Ashadha, Sravana, Dhanishta, Satabhisha, Uttara Bhadrapada, Revati*.
* **Business / Office Opening**:
  * *Ashwini, Rohini, Mrigashirsha, Pushya, Uttara Phalguni, Hasta, Chitra, Anuradha, Uttara Ashadha, Sravana, Revati*.

#### 2nd Priority: General Good Nakshatras (Fallback List)
If an event-specific star is not available within the desired calendar window, you may select from the **General Good Nakshatra List**:
1. **Swathi** (Swati)
2. **Chithirai** (Chitra)
3. **Magham** (Makha)

> [!WARNING]
> **Strict Rule**: If a star supports neither your specific event nor general auspicious activities, it displays `<font color='red'>Not recommended</font>` and should be **strictly avoided** for major beginnings. Move to another transit window.

---

### Step 4: Thithi (Lunar Day)

Evaluate the active Thithi in the transit window:

#### Auspicious Thithis:
* **Shukla & Krishna Paksha**: Dvithiyai (2), Trithiyai (3), Panchami (5), Sapthami (7), Dasami (10), Ekadasi (11), Trayodasi (13).
* **Pournami (Full Moon)** is highly auspicious for spiritual and peaceful rites.

#### Inauspicious Thithis (Highlighted in Red in Astrosoft):
* **Amavasya (New Moon)**: Complete absence of Moon's light; strictly avoided for auspicious beginnings.
* **Prathamai (1st Thithi)**: Dark/turbulent energy following New/Full Moon.
* **Ashtami (8th Thithi)** & **Navami (9th Thithi)**: Associated with conflict and challenges.

> [!TIP]
> **Timed Transitions**: If a row begins with an inauspicious Thithi (e.g. *Ashtami*) and transitions into an auspicious one (e.g. *Navami* or *Dasami*), the lower left pane displays the exact transition time: `Shukla Ashtami → (18 Oct, 10:21 PM PDT) Shukla Navami`. Use the **Subha Hora** selector or start instant after the transition.

### Step 5: Vara (Solar Weekday)

In Vedic astrology, the weekday (*Vara*) is measured **from local sunrise to the next sunrise** (Solar Day), not from midnight.

#### Auspicious Weekdays:
* **Monday (Chandra Vara)**: Gentle, nurturing, auspicious for marriage, family, and home.
* **Wednesday (Budha Vara)**: Intellect, commerce, education, contracts, and business.
* **Thursday (Guru Vara)**: Highly auspicious, expansive, wisdom, marriage, house warming.
* **Friday (Sukra Vara)**: Prosperity, love, vehicles, marriage, luxury, and entering a new home.

#### Inauspicious Weekdays (Highlighted in Red):
* **Tuesday (Mangala/Chevvai Vara)**: Fiery, aggressive, accident-prone, separation; strictly avoided for marriage and entering a new home.
* **Saturday (Sani Vara)**: Delays, obstacles, sorrow; avoided for auspicious celebrations.

#### Solar Day Awareness in Astrosoft:
* If your chosen event time is in the early morning before local sunrise (e.g., 05:30 AM), the solar day belongs to the **previous day**.
* The main table indicates this clearly: `Vara: Thursday → (07:05 AM PDT) Friday`.
* **Wednesday Caution**: Abhijit Muhurta on Wednesday is afflicted by Budha-Rahu dosha and displays `[Wednesday: additional scrutiny]`.

---

### Step 6: Amirthathi Yoga

Amirthathi Yoga is calculated from the specific combination of the **solar weekday at sunrise** and the **active Nakshatra**:

1. **Amirtha Yoga (Nectar)**: Highly auspicious; brings success, happiness, and long-lasting fruits.
2. **Siddha Yoga (Accomplishment)**: Auspicious; ensures the task is completed without impediments.
3. **Marana Yoga (Death/Destruction)**: **Strictly Avoided**; any activity commenced during Marana Yoga encounters failure, loss, or grief. Displayed in `<font color='red'>Marana</font>`.

Astrosoft displays the active Amirthathi Yoga and any timed transitions during the transit window.

---

### Step 7: Yogam (Nitya Yoga)

The 27 Nitya Yogas are formed by the combined angular longitudes of the Sun and Moon:

* **Auspicious Yogas**: *Priti, Ayushman, Saubhagya, Sobhana, Sukarma, Dhriti, Harshana, Shiva, Siddha, Sadhya, Subha, Sukla, Brahma, Indra*.
* **Inauspicious / Avoided Yogas**:
  * **Vaidhriti** and **Vyatipata**: Severely malefic; strictly avoided.
  * **Vishakambha, Atiganda, Shoola, Ganda, Vyaghata, Vajra, Parigha**: The initial portions (*Ghatikas*) of these yogas are particularly harmful.
* Check the Nitya Yoga line in the details pane to verify it is auspicious at your target hour.

---

### Step 8: Karnam (Karana)

A Karana is half of a lunar Thithi (6 degrees of lunar distance):

* **Auspicious Movable (Chara) Karanas**:
  * **Bava, Balava, Kaulava, Taitila, Garija, Vanija**: Suitable for marriage, construction, journeys, and general auspicious deeds.
* **Strictly Avoided Karana: Vishti (Bhadra)**:
  * *Vishti* represents poison and destruction. Initiating any auspicious undertaking (marriage, house warming, signing deals) during Vishti Karana is classically prohibited.
* **Fixed (Sthira) Karanas**:
  * *Sakuni, Chatushpada, Naga, Kimstughna* occur during Krishna Chaturdasi, Amavasya, and Shukla Prathamai and are reserved for spiritual or pacification rites, not domestic celebrations.

### Step 9: Muhurtha Chart (The Most Important)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Muhurta Chart is the single most decisive factor in electional astrology.** Even if the calendar day passes all previous 8 checks, an afflicted Lagna will ruin the election. Conversely, a powerfully fortified Lagna neutralizes minor calendar blemishes.

#### 1. Lagna (Ascendant) Selection & Strength:
* The rising sign (*Lagna*) must be strong and harmonious with the event:
  * Fixed signs (*Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius*) are ideal for **Grihapravesha (House Warming)** and long-term stability.
  * Dual signs (*Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces*) are favored for **Marriage**, education, and trade.
  * Movable signs (*Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn*) are suitable for travel and quick movement.
* **Lagna Lord**: Must be well-placed in a Kendra or Trikona, exalted or in own sign, and ideally aspecting the Lagna.

#### 2. Kendra & Trikona Suddhi (Benefics in Angles):
* Benefic planets—**Jupiter (Guru)**, **Venus (Sukra)**, **Mercury (Budha)**, and **Waxing Moon (Chandra)**—should occupy the **Kendra houses (1, 4, 7, 10)** and **Trikona houses (5, 9)**.
* Jupiter in Lagna or Kendra acts as a supreme protective shield (*"Lagna Kendre Gatau Jeevah sarva dosha haro bhavet"*).

#### 3. Dusthana Clearance (6, 8, 12 Free of Benefics):
* Benefics should **not** be placed in the 6th, 8th, or 12th houses.

#### 4. Malefic Placement (Upachaya 3, 6, 11):
* Malefic planets—**Sun (Surya)**, **Mars (Sevvai)**, **Saturn (Sani)**, and nodes (**Rahu/Ketu**)—should be placed in the **3rd, 6th, or 11th houses** (Upachaya houses), where they suppress enemies, destroy obstacles, and bestow courage.

#### 5. Ashtama Suddhi (8th House Purity - Non-negotiable):
* **The 8th house from the Muhurta Lagna must be completely empty.**
* No planet (benefic or malefic) should occupy the 8th house. An afflicted 8th house in a Muhurta chart causes vulnerability, sorrow, and shortened longevity of the endeavor.

#### 6. Saptama Suddhi (7th House Purity for Marriage):
* For **Vivaha (Marriage)**, the **7th house must be completely clean and free from any planets or malefic aspects**, safeguarding marital harmony and spouse well-being.

#### 7. Navamsa Suddhi:
* Verify the **Navamsa chart**:
  * Ensure the Navamsa Lagna and transit Moon are placed in auspicious signs (preferably Vargottama or benefic signs).
  * Avoid Pushkara Navamsa afflictions, 64th Navamsa, or Gandanta degrees (junctions between water and fire signs).

#### 8. Subha Hora Alignment:
* Use the **Subha Hora** dropdown in the lower right panel to snap your Muhurta to an auspicious planetary hour (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, or Moon Hora) during the chosen window, then click **Update** (which instantly updates the event date/time and the Muhurta chart).

## 3. Practical End-to-End Walkthrough

Here is how to apply this 9-step workflow in Astrosoft:

1. **Set Up Profiles**:
   * Click **[ Person 1: Select... ]** (and optionally **[ Person 2: Select... ]**) in the toolbar above the charts.
   * In the dialog, select an existing `.ash` horoscope file or use **Quick Entry** (enter Name, Rasi, and Nakshatra).
   * Once loaded, their natal Rasi charts appear next to the Muhurta chart, and the **[ Show Balam Calendar ]** button becomes active.
2. **Open Balam Calendar**:
   * Click **Show Balam Calendar** below the Muhurta chart to expand the monthly transit table.
3. **Filter Inauspicious Days (Steps 1 & 2)**:
   * Click the **Filter** button in the top right.
   * Days with Tara 7 (*Naidhana*) or Chandra 8 (*Ashtama*) for either individual disappear from the list.
4. **Select a Candidate Day & Window (Step 3)**:
   * Review the remaining rows. Click a row to load its details into the bottom split pane.
   * Check the lower left pane:
     * Review the list of approved activities displayed for the active transit star.
     * Confirm that the star supports the event you have in mind (e.g. *Marriage*, *House Warming*, *Travel*, etc.), or is one of the 3 general fallback stars (**Swathi**, **Chithirai**, **Magham**).
     * If it says `<font color='red'>Not recommended</font>`, move to another row.
5. **Verify Thithi, Vara, Amirthathi Yoga, Nitya Yoga, Karana (Steps 4 - 8)**:
   * **Thithi**: Confirm it is not Amavasya, Prathamai, Ashtami, or Navami (highlighted in red in Astrosoft).
   * **Vara**: Confirm it is not Tuesday or Saturday (red). Note sunrise transition if before 06:00/07:00 AM.
   * **Amirthathi Yoga**: Confirm it is **Amirtha** or **Siddha** (never red **Marana**).
   * **Yogam & Karana**: Confirm the yoga is auspicious and Karana is not **Vishti (Bhadra)**.
6. **Pick Subha Hora & Snap Time (Step 9 Setup)**:
   * Look at the lower right pane: select the preferred **Subha Hora** from the dropdown.
   * Click **Update**. The main event date/time instantly synchronizes to that Subha Hora. Muhurta chart is also updated.
7. **Inspect the Muhurta Chart (Step 9)**:
   * Examine the **Muhurta Rasi Chart** in the top left:
     * Is the Lagna sign auspicious and fixed/dual?
     * Are benefics in 1, 4, 5, 7, 9, 10?
     * Are malefics in 3, 6, 11?
     * Is the **8th house completely empty (Ashtama Suddhi)**?
     * If marriage, is the **7th house empty (Saptama Suddhi)**?
   * Compare with the adjacent **Person 1 / Person 2 Rasi** charts to ensure harmony with the partners' natal ascendants and moon signs.
8. **Final Confirmation & Summary**:
   * Adjust minutes slightly using **[ Edit Date/Time ]** if needed to achieve an ideal rising degree while staying inside the Subha Hora.
   * Click **[ Summarize ]** at the bottom of the left table to open a formatted plain-text summary of the chosen Muhurta, Panchanga parameters, and planetary positions for copying or printing.
